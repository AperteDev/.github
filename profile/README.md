# Aperte

Open source apps for GNOME that keep your data in your own Nextcloud.

## Projects

- [Notav](https://github.com/AperteDev/notav) - notes and a journal for GNOME, kept in your Nextcloud
- [Todav](https://github.com/AperteDev/todav) - tasks and calendar for GNOME, kept in your Nextcloud over CalDAV, with a GNOME Shell extension
